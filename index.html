How it works

Three steps. Zero hassle.

From your spare room to sold — without writing a single listing or meeting a single buyer.

01
📝
Register your items

Fill out the form below. Tell us roughly what you have, your area of Christchurch, and when suits you. No perfect photos or descriptions needed at this stage.

02
🤝

A vetted Cluttr reseller is matched to your collection. They contact you, arrange a collection time that works, then handle all the listing, selling, and buyer communications.
