About us

A better way to
clear it out.

Cluttr was born out of a simple frustration — clearing out a home is hard work, and most people don't have the time, energy, or know-how to get good money for their stuff.

We're a Christchurch-based startup building a marketplace that connects homeowners with skilled local resellers. You get to declutter without lifting a finger. Resellers get a steady stream of quality stock. Everyone wins.

Our resellers are vetted, local, and motivated — they only earn when your items sell, which means they're just as invested in getting you a great result as you are.

We're starting right here in Ōtautahi, and we're just getting started.
